Backend steps

How 1:1 backing is kept

The same five steps run for every tokenization. If any step fails, nothing after it happens and no money moves.

1 · GatewayChecks who is asking and that the form is valid.
2 · Transaction controlAsks COBOL to lock the dollars, so they can't be spent twice.
3 · COBOL ledgerMoves the dollars from the client's balance into the omnibus reserve.
4 · Tokenization serviceSmart contract mints tokens ≤ dollars locked.
5 · Event queueRecords the mint; COBOL's token record updates at the next batch.

Event queue · middleware

Every blockchain movement becomes an event. Events wait here until the COBOL ledger is online and the next batch runs. Each event carries an idempotency key (its block hash), so it can only ever be booked once.
